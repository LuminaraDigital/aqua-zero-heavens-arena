How a fighter is built. One 75-byte record each: fifteen 4-byte cards - power, type, flags, reserved - followed by fifteen growth-order slots that decide which card is learned at which level. Twenty-one records drive the whole roster, and the champion fights on a sealed five-card record that sits outside the table. Slots 12-14 are the air-combo tiers. The table above always shows the record the current fight is actually using.

How a fight works. Strike beats throw, throw breaks guard, guard stops strikes - and two strikes of equal power both land. On top of that core sit focus abilities, chip damage, per-fighter traits, three difficulty tiers, survival mode, hot-seat two-player and New Game+. The roster runs 25 fighters across 21 data decks, so four fighters double up; those four are separated by traits derived from their own dossier ratings, and every shared deck is labelled above.

Aqua Zero Heavens Arena, the AZX Fighting League and all 25 fighters are original properties of Luminara Digital.
